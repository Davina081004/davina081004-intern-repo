## Research & Learn

**Common cyber security threats in remote work**
- Phishing emails and fake links.
- Malware from unsafe downloads or out-of-date software.
- Unsecured wifi.

**Best practices for secure devices and accounts**
- Use strong passwords for each account, and turn on 2FA.
- Avoid public wifi for work.
- Only download software and files from trusted sources.

**Importance of locking computer when away**
- Anyone nearby could see or use my work and user data. Locking it stops accidental or unauthorised access.

**Handling phishing and suspicious links**
- Do not click or open anything that feels off, and check the sender’s real email address.
- Report it to my supervisor or team and delete it.

**Strong passwords and password managers**
- A strong password is long (12+ characters), uses a mix of words, numbers and symbols.
- A password manager remembers them for me, so I do not reuse passwords or write them down.

**Why 2FA matters and when to enable it**
- Even if someone steals my password, they still need a second step, like a code on my phone. 
- I should turn it on for email, work accounts, GitHub, Discord and anything with sensitive data.

## Reflection

**Security measures I follow now, and where to improve**
I lock my laptop whenever I am away from it, and also I use 2FA on some accounts. To improve, I can use a password manager and turn on 2FA everywhere.

**Making secure behaviour a habit instead of just a thought** 
Lock my screen every time I stand up, turn on auto-lock, and use a password manager so the safe way is also the easy way.

**Steps to ensure my passwords and accounts secure**
Use strong passwords, store them in a password manager, and turn on 2FA on all work accounts.

**If I suspected a breach or suspicious activity** 
Change my password, log out of other devices, and tell my supervisor straight away.
