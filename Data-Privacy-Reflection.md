## Research & Learn

**Key takeaways from the privacy policies**
- Focus Bear only collects data it needs, and follows GDPR.
- Habit data is double encrypted, and survey answers are only looked at in aggregate.
- Card details are handled by Stripe, so Focus Bear does not store them.
- Third-party services like AWS and Auth0 process data for them, and OpenAI is used for the daily motivational message.
- Users can ask to see, fix or delete their data by emailing privacy@focusbear.io.

**Data that are considered confidential at Focus Bear**
- Personal details like emails, phone numbers and login details.
- Habit data, because it can hint at religion or health.
- Survey answers (like ADHD or autism).
- Device info and error logs, payment and purchase history, and internal company information.

**Best practice for handling confidential data**
- Only look at data I need for my task.
- Use strong passwords. 
- Careful with my data, do not share data in the wrong place like a public chat.

**Response to a suspected data breach**
- Tell my supervisor or the team immediately.
- Stop sharing the data and write down what happened.
- Use privacy@focusbear.io as the privacy contact. 

## Reflection

**Steps for handling data securely day to day**
Only open what I need, use 2FA, lock my laptop, and double check who I am sending things to.

**Ways to store, share and dispose sensitve information safely**
Store data only in approved tools, share it only with people who need it, and delete downloaded files and screenshots once I am done.

**Common mistakes that lead to privacy issues and how to avoid them** 
Sending information to the wrong person, leaving a screen unlocked, using weak passwords, and leaving personal info in screenshots. I can avoid these by double checking before I send and by locking my screen.
