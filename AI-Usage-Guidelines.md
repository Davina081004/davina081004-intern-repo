## Research & Learn

**AI tools used for my role**
- I usually use Claude to expand my vocabularies, check my grammar and help write clearer reports.
- There is AI note takers for meetings, which the supervisor guide mentions.

**Benefits and risks of AI at work**
- Benefits: it saves time, explains things quickly and helps with writing and brainstorming.
- Risks: it can be wrong or make things up, it can leak private information, and I could stop thinking for myself if I rely on it too much.

**Information that should never go into AI tools**
- User data like emails, names, phone numbers, habits and survey answers.
- Passwords, login details and API keys.
- Internal company information and personal information

**Fact-checking AI content**
- Check it against official or trusted sources.
- Test any code or steps myself, and ask a teammate or supervisor if I’m not sure.

## Reflection

**When to use AI and when to use my own skills**
- Use AI for explaining basics, fixing grammar and getting ideas. 
- Use my own skills for testing the app, judging usability and making decisions.

**Ways to avoid over-reliance**
- Try the task myself first, use AI as a helper and not the answer, and always read and edit what it gives me.

**Ways to ensure data privacy**
Remove names, emails and personal details before pasting anything, and never paste confidential company or user data.
