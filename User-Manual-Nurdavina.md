## Research & learn

**What it user manual**
A short guide about how we work, so teammates know how to work with each other.

**Helpful categories to include** 
Communication style, work habits, work timing and location, how we receive feedback, and what helps us focus.

**How it helps teamwork** 
It avoids guessing and misunderstandings, because people know your style from day one. It is also easier to work with people around us.

## User Manual for Nurdavina

**My role**
- I am working on usability testing and finding bugs as a Usability Tester & Bug Hunter intern.
- The days and times I work are: Monday to Friday 1-2 hours per day (5-10 hours per week). However the hours of working might change if I am not available at some days.

**How I Work Best**
- I do my best work when I know what is expected, when the deadline is and also clear instructions.
- Flexible working timing and location also works well with me.
- I struggle when there are no clear indication on how this work will go in the future.

**Communication Style**
- I like kind and encouraging words as I sometimes will take critics personally. I do not mind long explanations as long as it is clear and simple.
- The best way to reach me is email.
- If you need something urgent, you can email or just give me a call.

**Collaboration & Teamwork**
- I work best in a team when everyone shares updates and communicates openly.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is being MIA or working with a passive teammate or team lead.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am brainstorming ideas to share.

**Focus & Productivity**
- I stay focused by setting short but continuous working time and taking regular breaks (snacking and stretching), I also keep my todo list and sticky notes nearby to keep tabs on myself.
- Things that distract me: social media, snacks, noise
- The best way to support my productivity is to give me clear tasks instructions and a clear deadline.

**Feedback & Growth**
- I like feedback to be clear and specific (does not have to be short), with an example if possible.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is directly and kindly, using encouraging words instead of harsh tone.
- I appreciate it when teammates tell me early, so I can fix things quickly.

**Fun Facts & Quirks**
- A fun fact about me: I was Design Director of my university’s Malaysian Association.
- My work superpower is getting everything done first before doing anything relaxing, therefore I do not usually procrastinate a lot.
