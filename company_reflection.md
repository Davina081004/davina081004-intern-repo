**Why do you think Focus Bear was created?**
I think Focus Bear was created to help people who is struggling with focus especially with neurodivergent people. Focus Bear is here to show people that being focus or consistent does not have to be difficult. 

**What problem is Focus Bear solving?**
Focus Bear solves short attention span issues, distraction problems and also helping people to build new habits and be consistent with it.

**Why do you think this mission is important?**
As technologies and AI are getting better, more people are dependant on the convenience and quick dopamine hits from it. Therefore even people without ADHD are starting to get similar symptoms and find themselves getting difficult to focus. So it is important that Focus Bear may help people to improve this issue.

**How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?**
I am interested in how social media today can affect young people's attention span and also how to maintain balance between social media breaks and getting work done. 

**Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?**
Yes, nowadays I find it hard to focus and have a short attention span. I often scroll social media when I am bored even there is nothing to see left on my phone.
